\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

DenoiseOpt repairs wavetable wrap clicks with compact operators searched by hybrid GA+PPO(+PBT) under locked $R_{\mathrm{blend}}$ and latency-aware $J$.
We reuse existing search tools; we do not introduce a new NAS algorithm.
Noise2Noise is the neural gate. Dual Cosine is the classical fade.

On five holdout seeds, short-FitCell hybrid scores $0.971{\pm}0.001$: below Noise2Noise ($0.977{\pm}0.001$), far above Dual Cosine ($0.542{\pm}0.038$).
Multi-family board means are statistically indistinguishable from Noise2Noise.
Hard cliffs collapse classical fades while searched operators hold.
A matched $5$k bake-off gives hybrid the top descriptive mean ($0.9748{\pm}0.0018$; $n{=}3$ search seeds; vs runner-up $\Delta{=}{+}0.0114$, $p{=}0.25$), so compute goes to hybrid-only FitCell-to-plateau without treating the ranking as formal superiority.
The FitCell-to-plateau $R_{\mathrm{blend}}{\approx}0.9912$ headline is \textbf{provisional} (single search seed, in-loop; Section~\ref{sec:v14-converge}), not a five-seed holdout result.
Cross-domain transfer boards are wrap-protocol stress tests (Appendix~\ref{app:transfer-bundle}).
No formal listening study was run; the claim is objective residual.
